An overview of how a DAEMUN III committee session actually runs. The full
Rules of Procedure and the Points and Motions guide (with Korean explanations)
are available under [Documents](#downloads).

## Before the conference

Lobbying, merging and the Approval Panel all happen **before** the conference
days. Blocs form, draft resolutions are merged and checked, and the approved
resolutions are what the committee debates on the day. Check the
[schedule](/#schedule) for the submission deadline.

## The flow of a session

1. **Roll call** — the chair calls each delegation, which raises its placard
   and answers “present” (or “present and voting” during a debating session).
2. **Opening speeches** — every delegate delivers a prepared opening speech
   (max. 90 seconds) in alphabetical order of delegation: who you represent,
   your country’s position on the agenda and your priorities. A template is
   under [Documents](#downloads).
3. **Main submitter** — for each topic, the main submitter of the approved
   resolution reads out the operative clauses, takes Points of Clarification,
   delivers the main-submitter speech (max. 3 minutes), takes Points of
   Information, then yields the floor back to the chair or to one other
   delegate. The floor cannot be yielded twice.
4. **Open floor** — the chair opens the floor. Raise your placard to deliver a
   general speech (no time limit) or to present an approved amendment. After
   every speech you must take at least one Point of Information.
5. **Amendments** — hand your amendment sheet to an admin; the chair approves
   it before it can be entertained. Once called, you have one minute to
   present it, then time *for* the amendment, then time *against*, each with
   Points of Information. The committee votes by placard — in favour or
   against, **no abstentions** on amendments.
6. **Voting on the resolution** — once debate time is exhausted, the committee
   votes on the draft resolution as amended: in favour, against or abstain.
   The procedure is then repeated for topics 1 to 4.

## Points you can raise

| Point | When to use it |
| --- | --- |
| Point of Clarification | Ask the main submitter about a term, abbreviation or sentence in the resolution. |
| Point of Information | Ask a delegate a question after their speech. A follow-up may be granted by the chair. |
| Point of Information to the Chair | Ask the chair anything, not only about procedure. |
| Point of Parliamentary Inquiry | Ask the chair about the rules of procedure while the floor is open. |
| Point of Order | The chair has made a mistake in running the committee. |
| Point of Personal Privilege | You are uncomfortable — you cannot hear, the room is too cold. Only audibility may interrupt a speaker. |

## Motions

Motions need a second and can be objected to.

| Motion | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Motion to Extend Points of Information | The delegate on the floor takes more questions. |
| Motion to Extend Debate Time | Extends the allotted debate time. |
| Motion to Move to the Previous Question | Ends the current stage and moves on — usually from time *for* to time *against* an amendment. |
| Motion to Move into Voting Procedure | Skips straight to the vote. |

## Speak in third person

Always “the delegate of Kenya believes”, never “I think”. You represent a
state, not yourself — even when its position is not your own.

## Conference rules

- Attend both conference days, give at least one speech and ask at least two
  Points of Information to qualify for the participation award.
- Notes go through the admins, in English only.
- No internet or AI use during sessions — admins check that Wi-Fi is off.
- School uniform or formal dress for the whole conference.
