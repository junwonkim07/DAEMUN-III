Descriptions adapted from the official UN websites.

## ECOSOC — Economic and Social Council

The principal body for coordination, policy review, policy dialogue and
recommendations on economic, social and environmental issues.

Source: [ecosoc.un.org/en/about-us](https://ecosoc.un.org/en/about-us)

## UNOOSA — Office for Outer Space Affairs

Promotes international cooperation in the peaceful use and exploration of
space, and the use of space science and technology for sustainable
development.

Source: [unoosa.org/oosa/en/aboutus](https://www.unoosa.org/oosa/en/aboutus/index.html)
